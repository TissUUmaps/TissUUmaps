Third-party software bundled with @tissuumaps/storage
=====================================================

The published files of @tissuumaps/storage include code from the third-party
packages listed below, either in its entry points or in its inline web workers.
Its other dependencies are installed alongside it and are not included here. The
license texts of the bundled packages follow.

Contents:

  - fzstd 0.1.1 (MIT)
  - geotiff 3.0.5 (MIT)
  - geotiff-tilesource 2.3.2 (MIT) [fork]
  - h5wasm 0.10.3 (NIST-Software)
  - hyparquet 1.29.1 (MIT)
  - hyparquet-compressors 1.1.1 (MIT)
  - hysnappy 1.0.0 (MIT)
  - lerc 3.0.0 (Apache-2.0)
  - pako 2.2.0 (MIT AND Zlib)
  - @zarrita/storage 0.2.0 (MIT) [fork]
  - zstddec 0.2.0 (MIT AND BSD-3-Clause)

Third-party code embedded in the packages above:

  - JPEG decoder (jpgjs) (Apache-2.0), in geotiff
  - HDF5 2.0.0 (WebAssembly build) (BSD-3-Clause-style HDF5 license), in h5wasm
  - zlib (part of the HDF5 WebAssembly build) (Zlib), in h5wasm
  - libaec (szip, part of the HDF5 WebAssembly build) (BSD-2-Clause), in h5wasm
  - snappyjs (MIT), in hyparquet
  - Brotli decoder (adapted from brotli.js) (MIT AND Apache-2.0), in hyparquet-compressors
  - gzip decoder (adapted from fflate) (MIT), in hyparquet-compressors
  - zlib (ported) (Zlib), in pako
