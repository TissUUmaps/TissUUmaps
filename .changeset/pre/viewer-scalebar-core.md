Projects get the optional `physicalUnitSize` (the size of one world unit, in `physicalUnit`), `physicalUnit` and `scalebarVisibility`, set with the `setPhysicalUnitSize`, `setPhysicalUnit` and `setScalebarVisibility` store actions. `physicalUnit` is free text; `PhysicalUnit` lists the suggested units, from picometers to kilometers, named as in OME-NGFF, and `physicalUnits` gives the symbol and the size in meters of each.
