Projects get the optional `worldUnitSize` (the size of one world unit, in `lengthUnit`), `lengthUnit` and `scaleBarVisible`, set with the `setWorldUnitSize`, `setLengthUnit` and `setScaleBarVisible` store actions. `LengthUnit` lists the units, from picometers to kilometers, named as in OME-NGFF.
